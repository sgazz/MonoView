MonoView

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains how MonoView, developed by Stanko Gazibarić, handles information in the iOS app and through its support website. Contact: sgazza@outlook.com.

Photos and recognized text

You choose photos using Apple's system photo picker. MonoView processes the selected image and recognizes text on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Reading aloud uses Apple's system speech synthesis. MonoView does not upload your photos or recognized text to the developer or an external AI service.

The app holds the working photo and recognized text in memory for its viewing and reading features; it does not create a persistent photo or recognized-text archive. Selecting a photo does not delete or modify the original in your photo library. Apple's photo picker may retrieve an iCloud photo according to your Apple settings.

Preferences and motion

MonoView stores preferences on your device, including your chosen hand, control positions, guide completion and viewing and reading settings. When you enable Tilt, device motion is used to navigate the photo. MonoView does not record a motion history or transmit motion readings to the developer.

No account, advertising or tracking

MonoView does not require an app account and contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. The developer does not operate a server that receives your in-app photos, recognized text, motion readings or usage events, and does not sell or use this information for advertising.

Optional support purchases

Optional one-time support purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app receives product and transaction information to verify and complete purchases and display their status. The developer does not receive your payment card details through MonoView. Apple maintains purchase records and may provide developers with sales and financial reports under its own terms and privacy policy. All app features remain available without payment.

Contacting support

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments are processed to answer your request and resolve the issue. Email is handled through Outlook/Microsoft and your email provider. Do not send private photos, payment details or sensitive personal information unless necessary for your request.

Support correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle your request, follow up on the issue and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request access, correction or deletion of correspondence by emailing sgazza@outlook.com. Any legally required retention will be explained in response.

Retention and your choices

You can change app preferences in Settings and stop using optional Tilt or speech features at any time. Deleting the app using iOS Delete App removes its local app data; offloading the app may retain that data. Device backups are controlled through your Apple settings. Removing MonoView does not delete your original photos or Apple's transaction records.

The developer has no uploaded photo, recognized-text or motion archive to delete. Contact support with privacy requests concerning information you have sent directly. Requests about Apple's payment records should be directed to Apple.

Website and platform services

The support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, tracking scripts or cookies to these pages. GitHub may process technical connection information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure hosting. Apple and Microsoft also process information when providing their respective platform, purchase and email services under their own privacy policies.

Children

MonoView does not ask users to provide their age or create an account. The developer does not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us to request its deletion.

Policy updates

Changes will be published on this page with an updated date. If the app's information handling changes, this policy will be updated to reflect it.
