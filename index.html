Getting started

  1. Follow the introductory guide and choose the hand you use.
  2. Import a photo using the photo picker.
  3. Use the zoom controls to enlarge it. Move the enlarged photo with touch, or enable Tilt to explore it by gently tilting your phone.
  4. Choose Read text to recognize text in the photo, open the reader and listen to it aloud.

Make it comfortable

Use Settings to adjust zoom, tilt and reading preferences. Press and hold the control panel’s grip to reposition it within the available area. The floating accessibility button can also be repositioned with a long press. Choose a position that feels comfortable without stretching.

Common questions

Text is missing or incorrect

Try a clearer photo with good lighting and larger, sharp text. Recognition may make mistakes with handwriting, small print or complex layouts. Compare the recognized text with your original photo.

The enlarged photo looks blurry

Zoom enlarges the detail already in the photo. Try importing a sharper photo or taking a closer picture.

How do I stop Tilt?

Tap the floating stop control. You can also adjust tilt sensitivity in Settings.

Do I need to pay?

All features are available without payment. Support MonoView in Settings offers optional one-time tips, with no subscription or feature unlocks. Apple handles the purchase.
